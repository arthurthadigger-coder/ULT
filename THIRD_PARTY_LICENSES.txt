   detune calibration to reach the requested +/-50 cents. See
   ThirdPartyLicenses/AIRWINDOWS-MIT.txt.
   Repository: https://github.com/airwindows/airwindows

   Author: David Braun.

   adaptation of its band curves, attack/release laws, threshold shifts and
   depth/time controls. Its LR4 crossover filters are implemented using JUCE.
   This is a source-level adaptation, not a bit-identical Faust-generated DSP.
   See ThirdPartyLicenses/FAUST-XFER-OTT-MIT.txt.
   Repository: https://github.com/grame-cncm/faustlibraries
